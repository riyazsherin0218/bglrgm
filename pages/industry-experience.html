Industry Experience Certificate

Proof of genuine workplace training, issued by the participating organisation and verifiable online.

Eligible students completing prescribed workplace training may receive an industry experience or training certificate from the participating organisation, where applicable. The certificate records the job role, the training period and the competencies demonstrated — the things an employer actually asks about.

On the Certificate

Information Recorded

  • Student name
  • Enrollment number
  • Organisation
  • Department
  • Job role / training area
  • Training period
  • Skills / competencies demonstrated
  • Completion status
  • Certificate number
  • Authorised signatory
  • QR / online verification

How It Works

From Placement to Certificate

1
Allocation

You are placed with a partner organisation in a defined job role.

2
Training & Log

Attendance, tasks and competencies are recorded and countersigned.

3
Supervisor Assessment

The workplace supervisor assesses performance and conduct.

4
Certificate Issue

The organisation issues the certificate with a verifiable number and QR.
