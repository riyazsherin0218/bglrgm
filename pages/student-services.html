Your Rights

What You Can Expect

  • A written statement of the certifying body before you pay any fee
  • An official receipt for every payment, with a verifiable receipt number
  • Internal assessment marks published before the external examination
  • A named supervisor and mentor during workplace training
  • A fair hearing before any disciplinary decision
  • Acknowledgement of any grievance within three working days
