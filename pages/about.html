Who We Are

Education Built Around Skills & Careers

BGLRGM Institute of Vocational Education & Training focuses on vocational education, practical learning, occupational skills and career development. The institution is operated by BGLRGM Institute of Vocational Education Training (OPC) Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 on 28 January 2025 (CIN U85306KA2025OPC197402), with its its registered office in Bengaluru, Karnataka and its corporate office in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

Our objective is to connect education with workplace requirements through structured learning, practical training, industry exposure and career-support activities. Programmes are built so that a learner finishes with something an employer can see: demonstrated skills, documented workplace hours and a verifiable record.

  • Vocational education across 15+ industry sectors
  • Practical and laboratory training as part of the curriculum
  • Internship, on-the-job training and Earn While You Learn placements
  • Career counselling, resume and interview preparation
  • Online verification for every certificate and record issued

Vision & Mission

What We Are Working Towards

🌅

Our Vision

To be a vocational education institution where every learner — whatever their starting qualification — can acquire a marketable occupational skill, real workplace experience and a career they can build on.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver practical, industry-aligned vocational programmes; to place learners in genuine workplaces during their studies; to keep education affordable through scholarships and Earn While You Learn; and to certify honestly, with every record verifiable online.

Nine Objectives That Shape Every Programme

Occupational Relevance

Design every programme around a defined job role and the competencies that role requires.

Practical Emphasis

Give practical, laboratory and field work the same weight as classroom instruction.

Academic Approach

How We Teach

Our academic approach starts from the job role rather than the textbook. For each programme we identify the tasks a person in that role actually performs, the competencies those tasks require, and the evidence that shows competence has been reached.

  • Competency-based curriculum mapped to occupational tasks
  • Continuous internal assessment alongside end-of-term examination
  • Practical records, log books and supervisor assessment as evidence
  • Employability and communication skills embedded in every programme
  • Curriculum reviewed with partner employers each session
Training Methodology

How Learning Is Delivered

Delivery blends classroom instruction with supervised practice and real workplace hours. For Earn While You Learn programmes the balance is approximately 70% industry exposure and 30% classroom learning.

  • Demonstration, guided practice and independent practice
  • Simulation and role-play before live workplace exposure
  • Rotational workplace postings with a named supervisor
  • Weekly training log reviewed by faculty and supervisor
  • Assessment of attitude and workplace behaviour, not only technical skill
