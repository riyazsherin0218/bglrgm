Hostel & Accommodation

Assistance in arranging student accommodation near your training location.

BGLRGM assists learners who relocate for a programme in finding accommodation close to their centre or workplace training location. Accommodation is arranged with independent providers; the institution does not itself operate a hostel at every centre.

Options

What May Be Available

  • Male accommodation
  • Female accommodation
  • Furnished rooms
  • Food / mess arrangement
  • Wi-Fi
  • Security
  • Transport accessibility
  • Accommodation near training locations
Availability, facilities and charges vary according to location and provider. Learners should inspect accommodation and satisfy themselves before making any payment. Payments for accommodation are made to the provider, not to the institution.

Accommodation Enquiry

Practical Advice

Before You Confirm a Room

👀

Inspect First

Visit the accommodation, meet the provider and see the actual room before paying.

🧾

Get It in Writing

Ask for a written statement of rent, deposit, notice period and what is included.

🚏

Check the Commute

Confirm the distance and bus route to your centre and to any workplace posting.

🔐

Check Security

Ask about entry timings, warden arrangements, lighting and visitor rules.

🍚

Check the Mess

Where food is included, ask to see the menu and timings, and mention any dietary requirement.

👨‍👩‍👧

Involve Family

For learners under 18 or moving away for the first time, involve a parent or guardian in the decision.
