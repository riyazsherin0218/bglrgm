Internal Assessment

How You Are Assessed

Assessment combines continuous internal assessment with an end-of-term external examination. Practical subjects are assessed on performance, record and viva.

ComponentWeightBasis
Internal assessment25 marksPeriodical tests, assignments, records, participation
External examination75 marksEnd-of-term theory or practical examination
Attendance requirement75%Per subject, to be eligible to appear

Internal marks are published on the student portal before the external examination.

Earn While You Learn
Practical Training

Facilities & Formats

🔬

Laboratory Sessions

Supervised bench work with a maintained practical record for laboratory and paramedical programmes.

🧑‍🍳

Skill Workshops

Kitchen, service, salon, workshop and technical practice sessions with demonstration and guided practice.

🏥

Clinical & Hospital Exposure

Observation and supervised practice at partner hospitals for healthcare programmes.

🏗️

Field Training

Site, farm, warehouse or store based training depending on the programme sector.

🚌

Industrial Visits

Structured visits to partner organisations with a reporting requirement.

📓

Log Books

Every workplace component is recorded in a log book countersigned by the supervisor.

Internship & OJT

Workplace Learning

📄

Allocation

Learners are allocated to a partner organisation according to programme, sector and centre.

👨‍🏫

Supervision

A named workplace supervisor and a faculty mentor track progress jointly.

🗓️

Duration

Ranges from a few weeks to a two-year workplace block under Earn While You Learn.

📝

Assessment

Supervisor assessment, log book and a completion report form part of the result.

🎖️

Certificate

Industry experience certificate issued by the organisation, where applicable.

🤝

Progression

Many learners are retained by the partner organisation after completion.
