Governance

Boards, Councils & Advisory Groups

Three bodies govern and advise the institution, each with a distinct remit.

The Board is responsible for the governance, compliance and strategic direction of BGLRGM Institute of Vocational Education Training (OPC) Private Limited. It sets the institution's direction, approves major decisions, and holds the executive leadership accountable for delivery against it.

Remit

  • Governance of the company and its statutory compliance
  • Approval of strategy, annual plans and major commitments
  • Appointment and oversight of the executive leadership
  • Financial oversight, audit and risk
  • Approval of new centres, partnerships and programme expansion
  • Custody of institutional policy, including the code of conduct

The Academic Council is the academic authority of the institution. It approves what is taught, how learners are assessed, and the regulations that govern academic progress and conduct.

Remit

  • Approval of curriculum, syllabi and programme structures
  • Assessment patterns, internal and practical assessment weightings
  • Academic regulations, attendance and progression rules
  • The academic calendar and examination schedule
  • Review of training methodology and learning outcomes
  • Recommendations on new programmes and sector coverage

The Industry Advisory Group brings partner employers — hospitals, hotels, companies, retail chains, laboratories and skill centres — into the design of our programmes, so that what is taught matches what workplaces actually require.

Remit

  • Advice on job roles, required skills and workplace standards
  • Review of curriculum currency against sector practice
  • Guidance on internship, OJT and apprenticeship design
  • Feedback on learner readiness and employability
  • Identification of emerging skills and new occupational areas
