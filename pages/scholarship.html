Up to 90% Merit Scholarship*

Scholarship opportunities may be available to eligible merit students. A scholarship is a concession against the tuition fee — not a cash payment — and it is not an entitlement.

Eligibility

What Is Considered

  • Academic merit — marks in the qualifying examination
  • Programme applied for and the places available in each category
  • Scholarship category notified for the session
  • Verification of original documents
  • Availability at the time the application is processed
  • Applicable terms in force for the session

*Scholarship percentage and availability are subject to the applicable scholarship policy.

Conditions

Keeping Your Scholarship

📅

Attendance

Maintain the minimum attendance prescribed for your programme.

📈

Performance

Pass internal assessments and examinations at the prescribed level.

🤝

Conduct

Adhere to the student code of conduct throughout the programme.

🏭

Training

Complete practical training, internship or OJT where the programme requires it.

A scholarship may be reduced or withdrawn where a condition is not met or where documents are found to be incorrect. Withdrawal is notified in writing and the balance fee becomes payable.
