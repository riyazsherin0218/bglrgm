Collaborations

State • National • International — academic, training, industry and placement collaborations.

How we describe a collaboration. Each relevant academic programme page identifies the awarding institution, the certification authority, the nature of the collaboration, the programme, the academic session and recognition or approval information where applicable. Claims are programme-specific and supported by current documentation, which prospective learners may ask to see before paying a fee.
🎓

Academic Collaborations

Arrangements with academic institutions for curriculum, delivery, assessment or certification of specified programmes.

🏛️

University Collaborations

Arrangements under which a legally authorised university admits, examines and awards a degree, with BGLRGM in a defined supporting role.

🇮🇳

National Collaborations

Collaborations with organisations and skill bodies operating across India for curriculum, assessment or training delivery.

🌐

International Collaborations

Cross-border arrangements for curriculum, faculty exchange, certification or placement, where established.

🏭

Industry Collaborations

Employer arrangements for training, internship, OJT, Earn While You Learn and recruitment.

🏫

Vocational Training Partners

Approved institutions and centres delivering BGLRGM programmes in their own locality.

🏥

Internship / OJT Partners

Organisations providing supervised workplace training places to our learners.

💼

Placement Partners

Employers who recruit from BGLRGM programmes.
