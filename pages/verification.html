Verification

Employers, institutions and learners can confirm any record issued by BGLRGM.

Demonstration mode. Verification runs against the sample records in assets/js/demo-data.js. In production these lookups should call an authenticated backend so that a record cannot be forged by editing the page.

Why This Matters

Verification Protects Everyone

🛡️

Against Forgery

A certificate that cannot be checked is a certificate that can be faked. Every record we issue carries a reference number.

⚡

Fast for Employers

No phone calls or letters — an employer confirms a candidate in seconds before making an offer.

🎓

Fair to Learners

Learners who did the work can prove it, anywhere, at any time.

If a verification returns no record, it does not automatically mean the document is false — it may not yet have been uploaded, or the reference may be mistyped. Contact us at and we will check the register directly.
