Bachelor of Vocation – B.Voc.*

Undergraduate vocational degree programmes offered through applicable recognised university arrangements.

Awarding institution: B.Voc. degrees are awarded only by the respective legally authorised university or institution. BGLRGM participates through an authorised academic, training or facilitation arrangement. The awarding institution, nature of the collaboration and academic session for each programme are confirmed in writing before admission — see the Recognition & Certification Disclosure.
⏳

Duration

Three years, ordinarily six semesters, with exit options where the awarding institution permits.

📋

Eligibility

12th pass or equivalent. ITI or diploma holders may be considered for lateral entry where permitted.

🏭

Structure

Skill components delivered with industry exposure alongside general education components.

How to apply

  1. Choose the specialisation and confirm eligibility
  2. Submit the online application with your 12th mark sheet
  3. Attend counselling and document verification at the centre
  4. Pay the applicable fee; GST extra where applicable
  5. Admission is confirmed by the awarding institution and enrollment details are issued

What to ask us before you pay

  • Which university or institution awards this degree?
  • What is BGLRGM’s role in the programme?
  • What is the academic session and mode of study?
  • What is the total fee, and what is payable each term?
  • Is this qualification suitable for the occupation I intend to enter?

We answer all of these in writing before an admission is confirmed.
