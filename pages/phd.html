Doctoral / Ph.D. Programme Information*

Guidance only. Doctoral degrees are awarded solely by the recognised university concerned.

Please read first. BGLRGM does not award doctoral degrees and does not represent that it can secure admission to any doctoral programme. This page provides information and admission guidance only. A Ph.D. is awarded solely by the recognised university that admits and examines the candidate, under that university's own regulations.

What guidance covers

  • Understanding eligibility requirements published by recognised universities
  • Identifying the vocational and applied disciplines relevant to your background
  • Preparing a research area and a preliminary proposal outline
  • Understanding entrance test, interview and coursework requirements
  • Understanding supervision, registration, progress review and submission timelines

Typical eligibility set by universities

Indicative only — the university's own regulations prevail.

  • A master’s degree in a relevant discipline, ordinarily with the minimum marks the university prescribes
  • A qualifying entrance examination or an exemption recognised by that university
  • An interview or presentation before the university’s research committee
  • Availability of a supervisor in the proposed area

How to proceed

  1. Speak to us about your qualification, experience and area of interest
  2. We share information on recognised universities offering doctoral programmes in that field
  3. You apply directly to the university and deal with it directly thereafter
  4. Any fee for a doctoral programme is paid to that university, never to BGLRGM
Caution: be wary of any offer of a doctoral degree without university admission, coursework, supervision and examination. If anyone claims to offer such a degree in BGLRGM's name, please report it to us immediately.
