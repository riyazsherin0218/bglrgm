About BGLRGM

Education Built Around Skills & Careers

BGLRGM Institute of Vocational Education & Training focuses on vocational education, practical learning, occupational skills and career development.

Our objective is to connect education with workplace requirements through structured learning, practical training, industry exposure and career-support activities — so that learners complete their programme with demonstrable competence, not only a certificate.
