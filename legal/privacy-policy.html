Privacy Policy

What personal information BGLRGM collects, why it is collected, and how it is handled.

Last updated: August 2026. This page forms part of the terms under which BGLRGM Institute of Vocational Education & Training provides its programmes and services.

1. Information we collect

  • Enquiry and application details — name, mobile, WhatsApp number, email, state, city, qualification, programme of interest
  • Admission records — certificates, identity proof, photograph and supporting documents you submit
  • Academic records — attendance, internal assessment, examination and result data
  • Fee and payment records — receipt and transaction references (card and bank credentials are never stored by us)
  • Internship, OJT and workplace training records maintained with partner organisations
  • Website usage information such as pages visited, collected in aggregate

2. Why we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and process applications
  • To deliver teaching, practical training, examinations and certification
  • To arrange internship, OJT, Earn While You Learn and placement opportunities
  • To issue receipts, certificates and verification responses
  • To meet statutory, tax and audit obligations

3. Sharing

Personal information is shared only with the awarding or certifying institution for your programme, with training and industry partners where you are allocated for internship, OJT or workplace training, with payment and technology service providers acting on our instructions, and with authorities where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

4. Verification pages

Public verification pages confirm the authenticity of a record against a reference number supplied by the person making the check. They display only the minimum details needed to confirm authenticity.

5. Retention

Academic and certification records are retained on a long-term basis so that qualifications remain verifiable. Enquiry data is retained only as long as needed for the admission cycle.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to correct your records, to stop marketing communication, or to explain how your data has been used, by writing to admissions@bglrgm.com. Some records must be retained even after a request, where law or certification requires it.

7. Security

Access to records is restricted by role. Staff, partners and employers see only the records relevant to their function. Report any suspected misuse of an account to us immediately.

Questions about this page may be sent to admissions@bglrgm.com or raised through the grievance redressal form.
